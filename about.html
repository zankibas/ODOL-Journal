About the Journal

About the Journal

Oral Dentistry and Odontology Letters (ODOL) is an international scholarly journal that focuses on the advancement and dissemination of scientific knowledge in dentistry, odontology, and oral health sciences. The journal serves as an academic platform for researchers, dental professionals, educators, and students to communicate scientific findings, exchange perspectives, and contribute to the continuous development of dental science and clinical practice.

ODOL welcomes scholarly manuscripts covering a broad range of topics within dentistry and oral healthcare, including restorative dentistry, dental materials, endodontics, periodontology, orthodontics, prosthodontics, oral and maxillofacial surgery, pediatric dentistry, oral pathology, preventive dentistry, community oral health, and emerging technologies in dental practice.

The journal is committed to promoting scientific integrity, academic excellence, and the dissemination of evidence-based research. It encourages original investigations, systematic and narrative reviews, clinical studies, and other relevant scholarly contributions that offer meaningful insights into the development of dental science and improvements in oral healthcare.

Through an international and interdisciplinary perspective, Oral Dentistry and Odontology Letters (ODOL) seeks to facilitate scholarly communication, strengthen collaboration among the global dental research community, and support the advancement of knowledge, innovation, and professional practice in dentistry.

The journal aspires to become a credible academic reference for the dissemination of scientific developments and the promotion of improved oral health outcomes worldwide.
